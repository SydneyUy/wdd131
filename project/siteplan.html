Abrahamic Family House - Site Plan

Site Name:

Abrahamic Family House

Site Purpose:

The purpose of the Abrahamic Family House is to provide a space for education and dialogue about the shared values and traditions of the Abrahamic religions.

Scenarios:

  • What can visitors see at the Abrahamic Family House?

    • Visitors can see beautiful displays of architecture that have symbolic significance in thier respective religions.

  • What services are available at the Abrahamic Family House?

    • The Abrahamic Family House offers educational programs, workshops, and tours.
